Jaden Bowers

About me

Jaden Bowers (b. 2003) is an oboist and composer. Bowers began playing the oboe at age 11 and later learned to play the alto saxophone in marching band. Soon after, he began to arrange and compose music for a variety of ensemble types. Bowers’s recent music, including the Saxophone Concerto in G minor and Partita for solo violin, was heavily inspired by the music of the Baroque era.
As a performer, Bowers has performed with a variety of ensembles, including the Anderson University Wind Symphony, the University of South Carolina Wind Ensemble and Symphony Orchestra, the Bowling Green Philharmonia, and chamber ensembles including reed quintet and wind quintet. Bowers graduated from the University of South Carolina with a Bachelor of Music in composition in 2026 and is currently attending Bowling Green State University to get a Master's of Music in oboe performance.
