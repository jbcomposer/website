Jaden Bowers

About me

Jaden Bowers (b. 2003) is an oboist and composer. Jaden graduated from the University of South Carolina with a Bachelor of Music in composition in 2026 and is currently attending Bowling Green State University to get a Master's of Music in oboe performance.
